Escalations & approvals

When an open milestone's delay against its original baseline reaches a rule's threshold, an escalation is raised and emailed to that rule's recipients with an approval link. Approvers accept the revised date or reject it and ask for a recovery plan. Escalations close on their own when the milestone completes or its delay falls back under the threshold; a higher level supersedes a lower one.

Update compliance

Each discipline must refresh the forecast of every open milestone once per cycle. The responsible person is emailed their update link when a cycle falls due. Missing a due date plus the grace period escalates to the discipline's Level 1 contact; missing two in a row escalates to Level 2. Escalations close automatically once the discipline updates.

Responsibility matrix

A discipline matches milestones by their category. Due day: 1 = Monday … 7 = Sunday for weekly updates, or the day of the month for monthly updates. Delay escalations also go to these contacts when a rule has "Notify discipline contacts" ticked: Level 1 to the responsible person and the L1 contact, Level 2 and above also to the L2 contact.

Escalation rules

Delay = days between the current forecast (or actual date) and the original baseline. A milestone is escalated to the highest level it reaches, once per level. Separate several email addresses with commas.
